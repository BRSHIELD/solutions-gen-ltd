## Services

### 1. Power Distribution
- **LV Main Distribution**: MDBs, SMDBs, final DBs, busducts, rising mains, metering cabinets, feeder pillars
- **Standby & Prime Power**: Diesel/gas generators, AMF/ATS panels, load-sharing & synchronising, fuel systems
- **Earthing & Lightning Protection**: Earth grids, chemical & maintenance-free electrodes, bonding, surge protection devices (SPDs)
- **Street & Area Lighting**: Highways, estates, industrial yards, sports facilities, smart/solar street lighting

### 2. Electrical Installations
- **Complete Wiring & Containment**: Trunking, trays, ladders, conduits, pulling, glanding, termination and labelling
- **Final Circuits & Accessories**: Sockets, lighting, emergency power, UPS circuits, clean power for ICT & medical
- **Mechanical Electrical**: Motor control centres (MCCs), VFDs/soft starters, pump & HVAC power, boiler rooms, chillers and plant rooms
- **Specialist Environments**: Hospitals (theatre IPS/UPS), data rooms, laboratories, cold rooms, clean rooms

### 3. Extra Low Voltage
- **Fire Detection & Life Safety**: Addressable/conventional fire alarm systems, voice evacuation, Nurse call systems
- **Security & ELV**: CCTV, access control, intruder alarm, electric fencing, intercom/PA, parking/barrier systems
- **ICT Infrastructure**: Structured cabling (Cat6/Cat6A/Fibre), data centres/server rooms, Wi-Fi systems, racks, ODF/MDF, active equipment installation and testing

### 4. Renewable Energy, Hybrid Power & Storage
- **Solar PV EPC**: Rooftop, ground-mount, carport - feasibility, design, procurement, installation, and O&M
- **Hybrid Systems**: PV + diesel generator + grid-tie with smart controls and load management
- **Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)**: Lithium-ion and advanced chemistries for peak-shaving and backup
- **Solar Street/Area Lighting**

### 5. Power Quality, Energy Efficiency & Audits
- **Energy Audits**: ISO 50002-aligned audits and conservation measures with ROI
- **Power Quality Analysis**: Harmonics, flicker, imbalance, transients—reporting and corrective actions
- **Power Factor Correction**: Automatic capacitor banks, detuned/reactor-based solutions for non-linear loads
- **Thermographic Inspections**: Of switchboards, busbars and terminations; corrective maintenance plans
- **Load Studies & Demand Management**: Peak lopping, demand response, tariff optimisation
