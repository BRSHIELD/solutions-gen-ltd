# Design Research: Solutions Gen Ltd Corporate Website

## Master Power Group Analysis
- **Color Scheme**: Red/crimson accents, dark blue, white, professional grays
- **Layout**: Asymmetric hero with diagonal design elements, modern engineering aesthetic
- **Typography**: Bold serif headings, clean sans-serif body text
- **Imagery**: Construction/engineering visuals, professional photography, geometric patterns
- **Navigation**: Clean top nav with dropdown menus, sticky header
- **Services Presentation**: Grid-based service cards with icons and descriptions
- **Call-to-Action**: Prominent blue CTA buttons with hover effects
- **Overall Feel**: Premium, trustworthy, innovation-focused, professional

## Solutions Gen Ltd Design Direction
**Premium Modern Corporate Engineering Aesthetic**

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Dark Blue (#1E3A5F or similar) - trust, professionalism
- **Accent 1**: Electric Green (#00D084 or similar) - innovation, energy
- **Accent 2**: Orange (#FF6B35 or similar) - energy, warmth
- **Neutral**: White, Light Gray (#F5F5F5), Dark Gray (#2D2D2D)
- **Dark Mode Support**: Dark backgrounds with light text

### Typography System
- **Display Font**: Bold, modern serif or geometric sans-serif for headings (e.g., Poppins Bold, Sora Bold)
- **Body Font**: Clean, readable sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Poppins Regular)
- **Hierarchy**: Large display for hero, medium for section titles, small for body

### Design Elements
- Geometric patterns and diagonal cuts
- Subtle gradients (blue to teal, orange to red)
- Professional photography of solar panels, electrical equipment, security systems
- Smooth animations and micro-interactions
- Sticky navbar with logo and navigation
- WhatsApp floating button for customer engagement
- Service cards with icons and hover effects
- Testimonial carousel
- Statistics with animated counters
- Featured projects showcase

### Layout Approach
- Asymmetric hero section with diagonal dividers
- Grid-based service cards (3-4 columns on desktop)
- Full-width sections with alternating backgrounds
- Sticky navigation for easy access
- Mobile-first responsive design

### Animation Philosophy
- Smooth scroll animations using Framer Motion
- Hover effects on cards and buttons
- Fade-in animations for sections on scroll
- Subtle parallax effects on hero
- Smooth transitions between pages

### Interaction Patterns
- Sticky navbar with smooth scroll behavior
- WhatsApp floating button (always accessible)
- Hover effects on all interactive elements
- Smooth page transitions
- Form validation with clear feedback
- Scroll-triggered animations
